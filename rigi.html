Group Activity Options

Mineralbad & Spa Rigi Kaltbad

The mineral bath at Rigi Kaltbad offers indoor and outdoor bathing with mountain views, as well as a separate spa area and bookable treatments.

Admission, treatments and other spa costs cannot be covered by the workshop and must be paid individually.

Visit the Mineralbad website ↗

Winter hiking

Mount Rigi has prepared winter hiking trails ranging from short walks to longer routes. Trail availability and difficulty depend on current mountain conditions.

Explore winter hiking ↗

Sledging

If there is enough snow and the routes are open, sledging is another option. Sledges can be rented on Mount Rigi when the runs are prepared and operating; rental is generally first come, first served.
