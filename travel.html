Arrival

Getting there

Please plan to arrive in Zürich well before 16:00 on Monday, 7 December. Please arrange your own journey from home to Vitznau; the group journey from Vitznau is coordinated separately.

What you need to arrange

01

Accommodation

No hotel booking is needed. Accommodation from 7 December through the final night you indicated in your registration—including the night of 9–10 or 10–11 December, as applicable—has been arranged and paid for. Any earlier or later nights must be arranged and paid for individually.

02

Travel to Vitznau

Please book and pay for your own travel from home to Vitznau. If you have difficulties booking public transport in Switzerland, please contact Maura.

03

Travel reimbursement

If you are claiming reimbursement for travel expenses, please complete the reimbursement form and return it to Maura with the required receipts.

Download reimbursement form ↓
04

Vitznau → Rigi Staffelhöhe

If you join the group journey, Maura will purchase this ticket for you. Please do not buy this section of the journey yourself.

At Zürich Airport

Allow enough time

If you are eligible and electronic passport-control gates are available, consider using them rather than staffed passport control; this may save considerable time.

For an international arrival, allow approximately three hours from your scheduled landing time to the 15:49 train. The 15:49 train from Zürich Flughafen to Zürich HB is the latest train that should be taken to catch the planned 16:05 connection from Zürich HB.

Public transport in Switzerland

Excellent and easy to use

Swiss public transport is excellent, so please use it in preference to taxis. Tickets can be bought in advance or on arrival from multilingual ticket machines, through the SBB app, or online.
