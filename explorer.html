Prototype explorer

Coming soon

The prototype is not yet available. It will provide a transparent environment for exploring how response-diversity metrics and assumptions can translate into predictions of ecosystem functional stability.

Workshop role

Scientific foundations first

The workshop is intended to define the conceptual and methodological basis of the explorer rather than present a finished tool. Discussion will focus on candidate response-diversity metrics, appropriate data sources, empirical validation, spatial extrapolation, uncertainty and use cases.

The longer-term aim is a spatially explicit explorer that can demonstrate how information on response diversity may support conservation, restoration, policy and management without implying a single universal stability index.
