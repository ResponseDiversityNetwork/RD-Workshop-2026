People

Participants

A focused group bringing expertise in response diversity, ecological stability, biodiversity forecasting, functional ecology, spatial modelling, remote sensing, ecosystem services, conservation science and quantitative ecology.

About these profiles. The short research descriptions and affiliations below are based on publicly available sources and are included only as a teaser to help participants get to know the group. Private registration information is not published.

Confirmed in person

Owen Petchey

University of Zurich · Switzerland

Professor of Predictive Ecology. Research on population, community and ecosystem dynamics, environmental change, ecological forecasting and statistical modelling.

Til Hämmig

University of Zurich · Switzerland

Junior researcher working on species environmental responses and temporal stability, including the relationship between response imbalance and community stability.

Maura Ganz

University of Zurich · Switzerland

Early-career biodiversity researcher working across biodiversity, ecosystem services, global change and conservation-relevant ecological assessment; developing a framework for spatially explicit ecosystem-stability forecasting through response diversity.

Gabriela Schaepman-Strub

University of Zurich · Switzerland

Professor of Earth System Science working on spatial ecology, remote sensing, biodiversity and ecosystem functioning across environmental gradients.

Thomas Johnson

University of Sheffield · United Kingdom

Biodiversity researcher using quantitative and qualitative approaches to study biodiversity change, its drivers and pathways to recovery, alongside interactions between people and nature.

Francesco Polazzo

University of Zurich · Switzerland

Ecologist working on response diversity, ecological stability, multiple environmental drivers and species interactions, with a focus on quantitative methods.

Maria Dornelas

University of St Andrews · United Kingdom

Professor of Biology and macroecologist studying biodiversity change through time and space, including synthesis of long-term community time series such as BioTIME.

Roslyn Henry

University of Aberdeen · United Kingdom

Interdisciplinary Fellow working at the interface of food security, land use and biodiversity, using global land-use and eco-evolutionary modelling.

Andrew Gonzalez

McGill University · Canada

Professor of Biology and conservation scientist studying global-change impacts on biodiversity, landscape connectivity, ecological networks and conservation action.

Samuel Ross

Kyoto University / OIST · Japan

Ecologist studying ecosystem resilience, response diversity and ecological stability using time series, experiments and landscape-scale monitoring.

Akira Mori

The University of Tokyo · Japan

Professor of Biodiversity and Ecosystem Services working across fundamental and applied ecology, forest science, biodiversity–function relationships and natural capital.

Robert Hechler

University of Toronto · Canada

Quantitative ecologist studying biodiversity dynamics and ecological stability, including nonlinear population fluctuations, time-series analysis and empirical dynamic modelling.

Miguel Bastos Araújo

CSIC / National Museum of Natural Sciences · Spain

Research Professor and biogeographer working on biodiversity forecasting, macroecology, climate-change impacts, species distributions and conservation modelling.

Laura Antão

University of Turku · Finland

Assistant Professor in Ecology and Evolutionary Biology studying biodiversity change, macroecology and global change using long-term data across taxa, habitats and scales.

Arnaud Sentis

INRAE · France

Researcher studying how temperature and other global-change drivers affect traits, trophic interactions, community dynamics and ecosystem functioning.

Stephan Munch

NOAA Southwest Fisheries Science Center / UC Santa Cruz · United States

Quantitative ecologist working on population dynamics, nonlinear ecological systems, empirical dynamic modelling and ecosystem forecasting for fisheries and management.
